# Design system

The dashboard is an operations tool used under pressure, sometimes during an incident. Its design rules exist so that a colour, a position or a badge means the same thing on every screen. Read this before changing UI.

## Where things live

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Colour and radius tokens, light and dark | `web/src/app/globals.css` (`:root` and `.dark`, exposed to Tailwind via `@theme`) |
| UI primitives (Button, Badge, Card, Table, Dialog, Dropdown, Segmented…) | `web/src/components/ui/` |
| Status vocabulary (renewal states, expiry levels, connectors) | `web/src/components/status.tsx` |
| Charts | `web/src/components/charts/` |

Components never use raw hex values. They reference roles (`bg-brand`, `text-muted-foreground`, `var(--series-auto)`), so light/dark mode and a fork's rebrand change in one file.

## Colour roles

The palette is called Signal and follows the 60-30-10 rule, in the spirit of Mobbin and Ramp: a quiet monochrome interface where colour comes from the product and one vivid accent. The landing page (`public/index.html`) and the app share it.

| Share | Role | Light | Dark | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60% | Ground and surfaces | `#ffffff`, `#f4f3f0` | `#0f0f0f`, `#171717` | Pages, cards, the warm-grey bands and trays behind product UI |
| 30% | Ink | `#141414` | `#f5f5f3` | Text, primary buttons, dark bands, the hero frame, the footer |
| 10% | Signal lime | `#c5f04a` | `#c5f04a` | Highlights only: the active row, glows, buttons on dark grounds. Always under black text, never as text or a link on white |

| Role | Light | Dark | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Series: automated | `#2a78d6` | `#3987e5` | Renewed by Farrier, in charts and connector tags |
| Series: manual | `#16956a` | `#199e70` | Installed by a person |
| Status: good | `#0ca30c` | `#0ca30c` | Verified, healthy, more than 30 days left |
| Status: warning | `#fab219` | `#fab219` | Under 30 days, awaiting a human |
| Status: serious | `#ec835a` | `#ec835a` | Under 7 days, rolled back |
| Status: critical | `#d03b3b` | `#d03b3b` | Expired, failed, errors |
| Neutral | `#8b8b94` | `#8b8b94` | Unknown, planned, superseded, cancelled |

Rules:

1. **Status colours are reserved.** They are never used for a data series, a decoration or a brand accent. Red always means something is wrong.
2. **Status never relies on colour alone.** Every status colour is paired with an icon and a text label, so it reads for colour-blind users, in greyscale and in forced-colours mode.
3. **Text uses text tokens.** Badge text uses the darker `*-fg` variant of its status (`--status-warning-fg` and so on) to meet contrast; the bright status colour is reserved for marks, dots and icons.
4. **Blue means automated, teal means a person installs it**, everywhere: the renewal calendar, coverage bars and connector tags. The pair is validated for colour-vision deficiency and for 3:1 contrast on white in both themes.
5. **Contrast first.** Body text is at least 7:1 on its ground, muted labels at least 4.5:1. Lime never carries text on a light ground.
6. **One primary button per view.** Near-black on light grounds, lime with black text on dark grounds.

Always render states with `<StateBadge>` and expiry with `<ExpiryBadge>`. Don't restyle them locally.

## Charts

- **Pick the form first.** A single headline number is a stat tile, not a chart. Ordered magnitudes are bars. Proportions of a whole are a segmented bar.
- **One axis.** Never a dual-axis chart. Two measures at different scales get two charts.
- **Fixed categorical order.** Automated is blue, manual is teal. Colour follows the entity, never its rank, and filters must not repaint survivors.
- **Projected values** use the same hue at reduced opacity, and the legend says so.
- **Thin marks.** Bars at most 18px wide, 4px rounded data end with a square baseline, a 2px surface gap between stacked segments, recessive gridlines.
- **Legends** whenever there are two or more series. A single series needs no legend; the title names it.
- **Hover tooltips** on every bar, with a hit target wider than the mark.
- **A table view** wherever a chart carries numbers someone might need exactly (see Forecast's Chart/Table toggle).

## UX principles in practice

| Principle | Where it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| **Jakob's law**: users expect your product to work like the ones they already use | A conventional app shell: left sidebar, top bar with status and the global action, content on the right. Tables sort by clicking headers |
| **Hick's law**: more choices mean slower decisions | One primary action per renewal row (Change pack, Retry or Advance), with the rest in a `⋯` menu. Add endpoint shows the five essential fields; windows, issuer and lead time sit behind a disclosure |
| **Miller's law**: chunk information | Sidebar grouped into Operate, Insights and Configure. Filters are a single segmented control with counts |
| **Fitts's law**: big, close targets are faster | Controls are at least 32px; nav items grow to 36px on touch layouts. Chart hover targets span the full bar slot, not just the bar |
| **Doherty threshold**: respond within 400 ms | Skeletons render immediately, actions give instant toast feedback, and data revalidates every 15 s and on focus |
| **Von Restorff effect**: the different thing gets noticed | One brand-coloured CTA per view. Overview leads with a "Needs you" queue, and the pending count badge sits in the nav |
| **Tesler's law**: complexity has to live somewhere, so put it in the system | Sensible defaults everywhere (port 443, `ec256`, any-time window), demo mode when no API is configured, connector config templates pre-filled from required keys |
| **Postel's law**: be liberal in what you accept | Discovery accepts hosts, CIDRs, port lists and `sni@ip` in one box, separated by commas or spaces, and validates strictly with a clear error |
| **Aesthetic-usability effect** | A restrained palette, consistent spacing and tabular numerals make dense tables easier to trust and scan |

Destructive actions (remove endpoint, cancel renewal) are styled in critical red, kept in menus, and removal asks for confirmation that states exactly what is and isn't affected.

## Accessibility

- Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in both themes. Status meaning is never carried by colour alone.
- Every interactive element has a visible focus ring (`--ring`) and is reachable by keyboard. Dialogs and menus use Radix primitives for focus management.
- Icon-only buttons have `aria-label`s. Charts have `role="img"` with an `aria-label`. Segmented filters are `radiogroup`s.
- Motion is minimal and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The theme follows the system by default, with a light/system/dark toggle in the sidebar and footer.

## Changing the design

1. Add or change a token in `globals.css` for **both** `:root` and `.dark`.
2. If you add a chart colour, validate it against both surfaces for colour-vision deficiency and contrast before using it.
3. Check the page in light and dark mode, at 375px wide, and with keyboard only.
